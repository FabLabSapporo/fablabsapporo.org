# Sapporo Creators Showcase 2024

- Date: <Badge type="tip" text="26.10.2024" /><Badge type="warning" text="10:00 - 17:00" />
- Event: [Sapporo Creators Showcase 2024](https://sapporo-elec.com/scs2024/)
- Venue: Sapporo, [Chi-ka-ho - Sapporo Ekimae-dori Underground Walkway  Kita-odori Intersection Square (West)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kJBrYvupgrBWmSBNA)

FabLab Kuriyama will join the [Sapporo Creators Showcase 2024](https://sapporo-elec.com/scs2024/) at the Sapporo Ekimae-dori Underground Walkway and we will be happy to talk about the [upcoming FabAcademy 2025](/en/fabacademy/). The event is organised by the [Sapporo Electronic Craft Club](https://sapporo-elec.com), it's a great chance to meet like-minded people with an intersted in electronics, fab & making.

This is also your chance to talk about to the Local FabAcademy Instructors - including me! 😁 We are happy to tell your more about FabAcademy, about it's content, our motivation for doing it - and what it's actually like, to be a student of FabAcademy!
